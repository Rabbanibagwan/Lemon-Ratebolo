Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Applies to prepaid Bag Balance purchases

This policy explains when Lemon Mandi Bag Balance purchases can or cannot be refunded, and how a customer can contact us for a refund or support request. It applies to prepaid digital Bag Balance sold by Rbolo Info Services Private Limited for the LEMON MANDI product.

1. What Bag Balance is

Bag Balance is a prepaid digital platform service for Lemon Mandi merchants. Purchased bags are credited to the merchant wallet and may be consumed by eligible platform usage inside the app. See Bag Balance & GST for pricing and tax information.

2. Orders and confirmation

A Bag Balance purchase is completed only after Lemon Mandi’s backend confirms the payment as successful (and, when a live payment provider is enabled, after that provider’s successful payment confirmation according to Lemon Mandi’s integration).

3. Cancellation before payment completion

If a payment is abandoned, fails, is declined, or is never confirmed, bag balance is not credited for that attempt. No cancellation fee applies to an incomplete payment.

4. After successful payment — when refunds are generally not available

Because Bag Balance is digital prepaid credit for platform usage, purchases are generally non-refundable once bags are credited to the merchant wallet, including when bags remain unused. Unused prepaid balance does not automatically create a cash refund entitlement.

5. When a refund or credit may be considered

Rbolo Info Services Private Limited may review and, where appropriate, approve a refund or corrective credit only in limited cases such as:

  • a proven duplicate charge for the same purchase,
  • bags credited due to a verified Lemon Mandi system error,
  • a payment captured without corresponding bag credit after investigation, or
  • a refund required under applicable law or expressly approved in writing by Rbolo Info Services Private Limited.

We do not promise automatic refunds for change of mind, unused bags, shop inactivity, staff mistakes in day-to-day mandi data entry, or dissatisfaction with unrelated mandi operations between merchants, farmers, or vendors.

6. How to request a refund or support review

Email support@ratebolo.com (or write to the registered office listed on Contact Us) and include:

  • shop / merchant name and Lemon Mandi account identity,
  • purchase / invoice reference,
  • payment reference (if any),
  • date and amount paid, and
  • a clear description of the issue.

We will review eligible requests using available billing and payment records. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

7. If a refund is approved

Approved refunds are initiated through the original payment method where possible, or another lawful method as applicable. Timelines depend on the payment provider and the customer’s bank or UPI app. Lemon Mandi cannot control third-party settlement delays.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated on this website. Merchants should review the latest version before making prepaid purchases.
