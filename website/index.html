LEMON MANDI

Lemon Mandi is a lemon auction and merchant management application for traders who handle farmer pattis, vendor bills, auction diary, bag balance, OCR capture, and day-to-day mandi reporting.

Contact / Get started Explore features

Built for lemon merchants who need one place to record auction lots, issue farmer pattis, prepare vendor bills, track prepaid bag balance, and review operational reports — from mobile devices used on the mandi floor.

Key features

Auction Diary

Record daily auction activity and lot details for the working day.

Farmer Patti

Create and print farmer settlement slips with rates, deductions, and net payable.

Vendor Bills

Prepare vendor bills with lot allocations, totals, and bank details where needed.

Bag Balance

Prepaid bag usage wallet for platform billing, purchases, invoices, and history.

Reports

Day and merchant-wise operational summaries for owners and staff.

OCR

Capture action-book pages and extract structured entries with assisted review.

Merchant Management

Shop profile, staff access, and platform administration for Lemon Mandi merchants.
