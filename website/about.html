About

Built for lemon mandi operations

Lemon Mandi is the product brand for a merchant application operated by Rbolo Info Services Private Limited.

Purpose

Lemon Mandi helps lemon merchants digitize day-to-day mandi work: recording auction lots, preparing farmer pattis, issuing vendor bills, tracking prepaid bag balance, running OCR-assisted capture, and reviewing reports.

Who it is for

The application is designed for merchant shops and their authorized staff who operate lemon auctions and related billing workflows.
