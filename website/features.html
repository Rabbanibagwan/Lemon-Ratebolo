Features

What merchants use every day

Lemon Mandi focuses on the core workflows of a lemon auction shop — from lot entry to settlement documents and prepaid bag usage.

Auction Diary

Capture auction lots and keep a working diary for the selected business date.

Farmer Patti

Generate farmer pattis with bag counts, rates, deductions, net payable, and print support.

Vendor Bills

Build vendor bills from purchase lots, with totals and optional bank details for settlement.

Bag Balance

Track prepaid bag balance, purchases, usage history, and tax invoices for platform billing.

Reports

Review operational summaries to understand daily activity across the shop.

OCR

Photograph action-book pages and use OCR assistance to speed up data entry with review.

Merchant Management

Manage shop details and authorized access for owners and staff using Lemon Mandi.
