Billing

Payment Information

How Lemon Mandi merchants pay for prepaid Bag Balance — a digital platform service — and how invoices and GST are handled.

What you pay for

Merchants may purchase prepaid Bag Balance inside Lemon Mandi. Bag Balance is a prepaid digital platform service for Lemon Mandi merchants (not a physical retail bag shipment). Full explanation, pricing reference, and GST details: Bag Balance & GST.

  • Reference price in default platform settings: ₹0.25 per bag (exclusive of tax)
  • Default GST: 18% (CGST+SGST or IGST as applicable)
  • Live price, GST rate, free allocation, and purchase availability are controlled by platform billing settings and shown in-app before payment

Payment methods

Bag Balance purchases inside Lemon Mandi are completed through secure checkout hosted by supported Indian payment aggregators. Depending on the checkout offered at purchase time, merchants may pay using UPI, debit or credit cards, net banking, or other methods shown on the payment screen.

This website describes how Bag Balance billing works for Lemon Mandi merchants. Payment credentials (UPI PIN, card PIN, or full card numbers) are entered only on the payment provider’s hosted checkout — not on this website and not with Lemon Mandi staff.

Confirmation and invoices

Bag balance is credited only after Lemon Mandi’s backend confirms a successful purchase. Tax invoices for paid Bag Balance purchases are available in the application after a purchase is marked paid. Supplier on invoices:

Security

When a payment provider is enabled, card/UPI credentials are entered on that provider’s secure checkout experience. Lemon Mandi does not ask merchants to share payment card PINs or UPI PINs with Lemon Mandi staff.
